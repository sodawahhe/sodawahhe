### Projects

**[Melbourne Parking & Congestion Analysis](https://github.com/sodawahhe/melbourne-parking-spark)** · PySpark, Spark SQL, Docker
Processed 72.9 M parking sensor records (9.2 GB) and cut the reporting query from 795 s to 230 s, a 3.46× speed-up, by finding via the Spark UI that the cost was a second full scan of the input rather than shuffle. Results verified identical with `exceptAll()` in both directions.

**[TikTok Virality: Cleaning & Feasibility Analysis](https://github.com/sodawahhe/tiktok-virality-prediction)** · R, tidyverse, ggplot2
Built a DAMA-aligned cleaning pipeline and an audience-size-independent definition of "viral", then tested whether the signal justifies a model. Content type separates strongly (median engagement 0.394 vs 0.259, p < 0.001); video duration barely moves it (~3%).

**[Australian Parrot Observations Dashboard](https://github.com/sodawahhe/endangered-parrots-shiny)** · R Shiny, Leaflet, ggplot2
Interactive dashboard over 39,486 observation records, with a Leaflet map and seasonal chart linked to species and season filters. Normalising each species to its own total keeps a species with 234 records legible next to one with 13,805.
